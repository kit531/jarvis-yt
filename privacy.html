Privacy Policy - Jarvis Youtube

Jarvis Youtube is used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not collect any data from other people.

What it uses: a Google sign-in token for the owner's channel, stored only on the owner's private home server, and the view statistics of the owner's own videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

What it does with it: uploads the owner's videos and shows the owner how they perform. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: contact the owner through the YouTube channel.
